User Data & Account Deletion

Last updated: October 12, 2026
Because Gravity Shift Arrows runs fully offline, there are no cloud accounts or server databases. All data is saved on your device, meaning you have full control and deletion is immediate.

How to Erase Your Game Data:

  • Inside the app: Go to the in-game profile screen and tap "Reset Profile". This instantly clears your nickname, unlocked levels, and star ratings.
  • Via Android settings: Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Gravity Shift Arrows → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
  • By uninstalling: Uninstalling the app completely purges all local storage files created by the game.
