Google Play Data Safety Compliance

User Data & Account Deletion

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Application: Gravity Shift Arrows

Google Play requires developers to inform users how they can request or perform the deletion of their accounts and associated data. Below is the full deletion policy and instructions for Gravity Shift Arrows.

1. Data Architecture

Gravity Shift Arrows is an entirely offline single-player game. The application does not create cloud accounts, does not maintain remote database records, and does not synchronize with any backend servers. Therefore, there is no remote server data retention.

2. How Users Can Delete Their Data

Because all data (profile nickname, progress, stars) is stored exclusively on your device, you have immediate, permanent control to delete your data at any time through any of the following methods:

Method 1: Reset Within App Settings

Open Gravity Shift Arrows → Tap on your Profile icon → Select "Reset Profile / Erase Data". This immediately resets all unlocked levels, scores, and nicknames.

Method 2: Android Device Storage Settings

Go to your phone’s Settings → Apps → Gravity Shift Arrows → Storage & cache → Clear Storage / Clear Data. This permanently purges all local app data.

Method 3: Uninstall Application

Press and hold the Gravity Shift Arrows app icon on your home screen or app drawer and tap Uninstall. Android completely erases the app’s internal sandbox storage.

3. Deletion Timeline

Because data deletion is handled client-side directly by the user on the device, deletion occurs immediately (0 seconds delay) without any pending approval or request processing required.

4. Assistance & Questions

If you have any questions regarding your data or need help, contact: support@gravityshiftarrows.app.
