Privacy Policy for Gravity Shift Arrows

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravity Shift Arrows ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy, and our application is designed from the ground up to operate completely offline.

Core Guarantee: Gravity Shift Arrows does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. No data ever leaves your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal data, sensitive user data, or telemetry:

  • No personal identification details (such as real name, email, phone number, address).
  • No location data (neither precise nor coarse GPS data).
  • No advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • No diagnostic crash logs, performance analytics, or behavioral telemetry sent to remote servers.
  • No internet communication: The application does not connect to any web API, database, or server.

2. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

The Application saves limited configuration data directly in your device’s local sandbox storage using standard Android storage mechanisms (e.g. SharedPreferences/SQLite):

  • Local Profile Name: An optional display name you choose to personalize your in-game interface.
  • Local Passcode / PIN (Hashed): If you choose to protect your local profile, a salted one-way hash is saved locally to verify your pin.
  • Game Progress & Preferences: Level completion records, unlocked stages, star ratings achieved, audio/vibration toggles, and high scores.

This data remains strictly on your smartphone or tablet. We have no physical or technical means to access it.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Gravity Shift Arrows does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising networks, payment gateways, or user tracking (such as Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, AdMob, or Unity Ads). The game is 100% ad-free.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because the Application collects no personal information whatsoever from any user, it fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Families Policy. It is safe for players of all ages.

5. Permissions

Gravity Shift Arrows requests zero dangerous or sensitive permissions on your device. It does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, storage files, or network state.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full ownership and direct control over your local data. You can delete all saved progress and profile information at any moment by:

  • Using the in-app profile reset option.
  • Clearing the app storage in your device's Settings → Apps → Gravity Shift Arrows → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the Application from your device.

7. Updates to This Policy

If we ever make modifications to this policy, the updated document will be published at this permanent URL with a revised effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our support email: support@gravityshiftarrows.app (or your designated developer contact email).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game require an active internet connection? +
No. Gravity Shift Arrows runs entirely offline on your phone. You can play on airplanes, subways, or anywhere without mobile network connectivity.
Are there any in-app purchases or paywalls? +
No. All 100+ levels and game features are completely free and unlocked through gameplay. There are no virtual currencies, lives, or microtransactions.
How do I rotate an arrow in reverse? +
Tap an arrow to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise. Long-press on an arrow (or right click on desktop) to rotate it counter-clockwise.
How is my 3-star rating calculated? +
Each level has a minimum move par. Completing the puzzle in fewer or equal moves earns 3 stars. You can replay any solved stage anytime to improve your score.
