Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 • Game: Gravity Shift Arrows
The short version: I built this game to be played offline. It doesn't connect to a server, doesn't use ads or tracking libraries, and doesn't collect any personal information from you.

1. What Information We Collect

None. We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location, or device ID. The game has no remote backend or telemetry servers.

2. What Is Saved On Your Phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves a few simple things locally on your device:

  • The nickname you enter on your profile screen.
  • A one-way salted hash of your optional profile PIN (if you choose to lock your profile).
  • Your puzzle progress (which levels you beat and your star scores).
  • Your sound and vibration settings.

This data stays inside your phone's sandbox storage. It never gets uploaded anywhere.

3. Ads and Analytics

Gravity Shift Arrows contains zero ads and zero third-party tracking SDKs (like Facebook SDK, Firebase, or Google AdMob). There are no background analytic trackers.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it is safe for all ages and complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policy requirements.

5. Device Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions (no camera, contacts, microphone, storage files, or GPS location).

6. How to Delete Your Data

You can wipe all saved scores and profile data anytime by tapping Reset Profile inside the game, clearing app data in your Android settings, or simply uninstalling the app.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, drop me an email at: support@gravityshiftarrows.app.

Data & Account Deletion

Last updated: October 12, 2026
Because Gravity Shift Arrows runs completely offline, there are no online accounts or cloud databases to delete from. Everything is saved only on your phone, so deleting it is instant.

How to Erase Your Saved Game Data:

  • In the game: Go to your Profile screen and tap "Reset Profile". All completed levels and star ratings will be cleared instantly.
  • From your phone settings: Open Settings → Apps → Gravity Shift Arrows → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
  • By uninstalling: Uninstalling the game completely wipes all local files created by the app.

Support & Player FAQ

Got questions or ran into a bug? We've got answers.

Email me directly at support@gravityshiftarrows.app. I try to reply to player questions and bug reports within 24 to 48 hours.

Do I need Wi-Fi or data to play?

Nope! The game is 100% offline. Great for flights, subways, or whenever you don't have signal.

Are there in-app purchases or paywalls?

No paywalls, no energy systems, and no microtransactions. All levels are unlocked purely by playing.

How do I turn an arrow the other way?

A quick tap turns it clockwise (90°). A long press rotates it counter-clockwise.

How do I get 3 stars on a level?

Solve the puzzle in fewer or equal moves to the par target. You can replay any completed stage to improve your score.
