Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026
In short: Gravity Shift Arrows is a completely offline single-player puzzle game. It does not collect, track, or share any personal data. No accounts, no ads, no internet connection required.

1. What Data We Collect

None. The game does not have an external server, does not connect to the internet, and does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or advertising frameworks.

2. What Is Stored Locally On Your Device

Everything you do in the game stays locally on your device in standard local app storage:

  • The nickname you choose when setting up your profile.
  • A one-way hashed version of your optional profile PIN.
  • Your level progress, star ratings, and sound settings.

This data stays on your phone and we have no access to it.

3. Advertisements & Trackers

There are no ads and no third-party tracking SDKs included in the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the game does not collect any personal data from anyone, it is fully safe for players of all ages, including children under 13.

5. Permissions

Gravity Shift Arrows asks for no sensitive permissions (no network access, camera, storage access, or location).

6. How to Delete Your Data

You can erase all your saves anytime by using the "Reset Profile" button in the game, clearing app storage in your Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

7. Contact

If you have any questions, feel free to email: support@gravityshiftarrows.app.
