Official Google Play Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Application: Gravity Shift Arrows

Core Guarantee: Gravity Shift Arrows does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personal information. It operates 100% offline.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android mobile game Gravity Shift Arrows ("the App", "we", "us"). The App is a single-player offline puzzle game designed for pure relaxation and logic problem solving without online telemetry or personal tracking.

2. Information We Collect

Zero (None). The App connects to no external servers, databases, or remote endpoints. We do not collect or log:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identity credentials.
  • No geographic location (neither coarse nor fine GPS data).
  • No device identifiers, IMEI numbers, or advertising identifiers (AAID).
  • No behavioral analytics, keystroke logging, or interaction tracking.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Application saves limited configuration data directly within your device’s private sandbox storage:

  • Display Name: An optional nickname you provide to customize your profile interface.
  • Passcode (Salted Hash): An optional passcode stored as a secure one-way hash to guard your local profile.
  • Game Progress: Solved levels, star ratings earned, and audio preferences.

This data never leaves your device and cannot be accessed by the developer or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application contains no advertisements and does not integrate third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook SDK, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).

5. Children's Privacy

Gravity Shift Arrows does not collect any personal data from any person, including children under 13. It complies with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

6. Permissions Requested

The App requests no sensitive runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, storage access, or location).

7. Data Deletion

Players retain 100% control over their local data. To erase all game progress and profile information:

  • Choose the reset data option in the game's profile screen.
  • Or navigate to Android Settings → Apps → Gravity Shift Arrows → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
  • Or uninstall the game from your device.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or require support, please contact the developer at: support@gravityshiftarrows.app.
