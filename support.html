Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the app collect any personal data or usage metrics?

No. Gravity Shift Arrows operates 100% offline and connects to no analytics, advertising, or telemetry servers.

Q: How do I rotate arrows in reverse?

A single tap rotates the arrow clockwise 90 degrees. A long press on the arrow rotates it 90 degrees counter-clockwise.

Q: How can I reset my game progress?

Open the in-game profile page and select "Reset Profile / Clear Data", or go to your device's Android App Settings and clear app storage.

Q: What Android versions are supported?

Gravity Shift Arrows is compatible with modern Android devices running Android 6.0 (API level 23) and above.
