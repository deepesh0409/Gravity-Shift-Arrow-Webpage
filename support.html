Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No, it’s 100% offline. You can play anywhere without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Are there any hidden ads or in-app purchases?
None. There are no ads, subscriptions, or paywalls. All levels are unlocked through gameplay.
How do I rotate an arrow in reverse?
A single tap turns an arrow clockwise. A long-press rotates it counter-clockwise.
How do I wipe my progress to start fresh?
Head over to your in-game profile screen and tap "Reset Profile", or clear app storage in your Android settings.
